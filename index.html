Paulina Bereza

Textile Design & Material Innovation

info

Paulina Bereza (b.1988) is a textile material designer with a focus on historical craft practices, and exploration of sustainable biomaterials. Her work draws upon her upbringing in southern Poland and Montreal, Quebec. She currently lives in the pacific northwest of the United States.
